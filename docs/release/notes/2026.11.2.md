## Highlights

- **Dictate while another app has focus.** Turn on Desktop integration under **Settings → Speech Kit → Capture** to use a global shortcut or a menu bar or tray control. Keep Obsidian running with the destination note open. On macOS, the popover shows the target note and capture state.
- **More local transcription models.** Try Qwen3 ASR 0.6B or 1.7B for multilingual file transcription and batch dictation, or Whisper Small Multilingual for a compact Whisper option. In dictation, Qwen3 ASR returns text after each phrase. The 1.7B model is a 2.52 GB download.

## Improvements

- **Choose what stays visible.** Put the dictation button in the ribbon, status bar, or both, or keep just commands and hotkeys. Use **Advanced → Customize tools** to hide translation, read-aloud, or AI tools and bring them back with their settings intact.
- **Clearer recording and transcript settings.** Phrase timing and preset choices are easier to read. Timestamp settings show examples, and speaker settings help you switch to a compatible batch model. Speaker labels are estimates you can review after transcription.

## Fixes

- **Keep track of text when something goes wrong.** Dictation warns if the editor refuses a write and releases the microphone if the local engine exits. File transcription keeps recoverable text when it can't insert it, and longer files are processed within the model's per-phrase limits.
- **Use the tools together.** Import available YouTube captions while dictating, browse for audio from a popout, cancel translation while it runs, and change read-aloud speed without resuming paused playback.

## Compatibility

- **Updated local engine.** When Speech Kit prompts you after updating, reinstall the local engine to get the new models and desktop controls. The [model and hardware matrix](https://github.com/brittain9/speech-kit-obsidian-plugin#models-and-acceleration) lists the CPU, Metal, and CUDA options.

## Known Limitations

- **Linux desktop controls depend on your session.** The global shortcut needs a supported desktop portal, and the tray icon needs a compatible tray host. See the [desktop controls guide](https://github.com/brittain9/speech-kit-obsidian-plugin/blob/main/docs/guides/desktop-controls.md#fedora-and-omarchy).
