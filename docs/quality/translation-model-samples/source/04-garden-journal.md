# Balcony garden journal

The tomatoes finally reached the top of the railing this week. Two plants are producing fruit, although the smaller one still has curled leaves after the hot weekend. I moved its pot away from the brick wall because the wall was radiating heat long after sunset.

## Today

- Watered the herbs at 07:30.
  - Basil: soil dry, leaves healthy.
  - Mint: too wet; skip tomorrow.
  - Rosemary: new growth near the south edge.
- Tied the tomato stems loosely with cloth.
- Removed three yellow leaves.
- Added a small label: `Roma — planted 5/14`.

> [!tip] Next heat wave
> Move the shallow pots into afternoon shade and check them twice. Do not add fertilizer merely because the leaves droop at noon.

The weather report predicts two cooler nights. If the temperature falls below 15°C, the seedlings can stay outside, but growth may slow. The rough watering estimate is $V = 0.1d^2$ liters for a round pot with diameter $d$ measured in centimeters; this is only a reminder, not a horticultural rule.
