# Monthly finance review

Spending was higher than planned, mostly because two annual bills landed in the same week. That is a timing issue, not evidence that the regular monthly budget is broken. The useful comparison is with the sinking-fund balance, not with an artificially smooth average.

## Snapshot

| Category  |   Plan | Actual | Note            |
| --------- | -----: | -----: | --------------- |
| Housing   | $2,100 | $2,100 | fixed           |
| Groceries |   $500 |   $574 | delivery fees   |
| Transport |   $180 |   $143 | below plan      |
| Software  |    $90 |   $212 | annual renewals |

> [!note] Interpretation
> Do not describe a transfer to savings as spending. It changes account location, not net worth.

Actions:

- Cancel one unused subscription before the next renewal.
- Compare grocery delivery totals with [[Food Spending Review]].
- Move the insurance renewal into a dedicated sinking fund.
- Keep the emergency fund target at six months; do not chase yield with money needed soon.

The simple savings rate is $s = (I - E) / I$, but the inputs need consistent treatment. Taxes, reimbursements, and internal transfers can make the number misleading if the categories drift.
