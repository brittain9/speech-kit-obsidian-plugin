# Three days in Lisbon

This is a loose plan, not a checklist. The priority is enough structure to avoid wasting mornings while leaving room to stay somewhere interesting.

## Friday — arrival

- Take the metro from the airport unless the luggage is awkward.
- Leave bags near [[Baixa]].
- Walk toward the river before dinner.
  - If it is raining, visit a small museum instead.
  - If the flight is late, skip the viewpoint.

> [!warning] Timing
> “Next Tuesday” and “this Tuesday” are not interchangeable. Confirm every reservation date in the original language before paying.

## Saturday — older neighborhoods

Start early in Alfama, then walk uphill slowly. The route is short on a map but steep in practice. Stop for coffee rather than trying to optimize every minute. In the afternoon, choose one of two options:

1. Continue toward Graça and the viewpoints.
2. Take a tram west and spend more time indoors.

## Sunday — flexible

Do not plan an early train unless everyone agrees the night before. A nearby market and a long lunch are acceptable. Buy no fragile souvenirs. #travel/portugal
